# Privacy Policy — CourtSide

**Last updated:** 25 July 2026
**App:** CourtSide (badminton scoring & club tracker)
**Developer:** Utkarsh Agrawal
**Contact:** utkarshg08@gmail.com

## Summary

CourtSide is an **offline-first** app. In this release it works entirely on your
device. We do **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, and
the app contains **no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs**.

## Data we collect

**None is collected by us.** All information you enter — player names, matches,
scores, sessions, attendance, and statistics — is stored **locally on your
device** (in an on-device SQLite database) and never leaves it in this version
of the app.

## Data we share

**None.** No data is sent to us or to any third party.

## Permissions

The app may use network/connectivity access to detect whether you are online.
This release does not upload any of your data; the capability exists to support
optional cloud sync in a future version. If cloud sync and accounts are
introduced, this policy will be updated **before** any data is transmitted, and
you will be able to review it.

## Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, **uninstalling the app permanently
deletes all of it**. There is no server copy for us to hold or delete.

## Children

CourtSide is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
any information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, when optional accounts/cloud sync are
added), the "Last updated" date above will change and the new terms will be
posted here.
