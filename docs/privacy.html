Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026
CourtSide is an offline-first app. In this release it works entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, and the app contains no marketing analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.

Data we collect

None is collected by us. All information you enter — player names, matches, scores, sessions, attendance, and statistics — is stored locally on your device and never leaves it in this version of the app.

Crash and error reporting

CourtSide uses Sentry to collect anonymized crash and error diagnostics (for example, a stack trace showing where the app failed) so we can find and fix bugs. This is limited to technical failure data — it does not include your player names, matches, scores, or other content, and any user identifier is stripped down to a random ID. It is not used for marketing, advertising, or tracking your activity.

Data we share

None, other than the anonymized crash diagnostics described above, which are sent only to our crash-reporting provider (Sentry) and used solely to improve app stability.

Permissions

The app may use network/connectivity access to detect whether you are online. This release does not upload any of your data; the capability exists to support optional cloud sync in a future version. If cloud sync and accounts are introduced, this policy will be updated before any data is transmitted.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of it. There is no server copy for us to hold or delete.

Children

CourtSide is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, when optional accounts and cloud sync are added), the "Last updated" date above will change and the new terms will be posted here.
